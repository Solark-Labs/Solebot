The process

Three gates before
anything touches your shoe.

Detection → assessment → consent. Only then does cleaning begin. It's a protocol, not a car wash.

01
Contamination gate
Mud scoring via depth geometry
A 3D depth sensor maps the sole in 2 seconds. Mud adds 3–8mm of irregular topology. Score 0–100 determines routing — not a photo, a geometry map.
0.5 bar · 60 Hz vibration
02
Material gate
NIR spectroscopy on the upper
Near-infrared light bounces off the upper and returns a molecular signature. Leather, suede, canvas, mesh, PU — each uniquely identifiable. Cannot be fooled by colour or lighting.
1680nm · 2300nm peaks
03
Consent gate
Quote → pay → 360° pre-capture
Price and time shown before cleaning. Customer pays. A full 360° video is recorded as the legal pre-service condition record. Only then does any mechanism touch the shoe.
Pre-service legal record
04
Cleaning
Adaptive protocol execution
The shoe rotates at 6 RPM on a honeycomb platform. A stationary brush head applies the right method. Pressure is servo-controlled at ±0.1 N. No guessing.
6 RPM · ±0.1 N control
05
Drying
Material-calibrated warm air
Canvas: 55°C, 4 minutes. Leather: 38°C, 6 minutes. Heat enters from above and below simultaneously. No infra-red, no direct heat contact.
38–55°C · dual-channel
06
Documentation
Post-service capture + delivery
Before/after video stored and sent to customer's phone. Service record includes method used, parameters logged, timestamp. Full accountability.
Before + after · cloud stored

Luxury mode

For the shoe that cost
more than a motorcycle.

When NIR detects patent leather, exotic hide, or nubuck — or the customer declares a luxury item — the entire cleaning physics changes. Not just softer. Different.

01
15 RPM goat-hair brush
Standard is 80 RPM nylon. Luxury mode uses genuine goat hair at 15 RPM — barely moving. The contact force is below what you feel on your own skin.
02
Zero water contact
No rinse, no mist. Foam is applied and lifted — never washed. A dry microfibre cloth removes residue. Waterlogging a Berluti sole destroys the welt stitching.
03
L-fork heel support
A mechanical fork slides under the heel while the gripper holds the upper. Weight is distributed — no stress on the sole-upper bond of structurally complex shoes.
04
Lanolin conditioner mist + wrapping
Post-clean, lanolin is misted onto the upper. The shoe is returned wrapped in acid-free tissue. The customer's shoe comes back in better condition than it arrived.
